    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 19, 2026

    This policy explains what information Passion Fruit collects when you use the support portal, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

    1. Who we are

    Passion Fruit Inc. ("we", "us") runs the Passion Fruit support portal, which offers troubleshooting guides, video and audio tutorials, and live chat for Fruit devices. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

    2. Information we collect

    • Account details: your first, middle (optional) and last name, your email address and your password.
    • Support activity: messages you send in live chat and the guides you search for or open, so we can help you and improve our content.
    • Information stored on your device: your theme choice (light or dark) and, if you ask us to, your email address so you can sign in faster. See "Cookies and local storage" below.
    • Technical data: basic details such as browser type and device type, used to keep the portal secure and working properly.

    3. How we use it

    • to create and manage your account and let you sign in;
    • to answer your questions and provide support;
    • to keep the portal secure and prevent misuse;
    • to understand which guides help most and make them better;
    • to send important service messages, such as account or security notices.

    4. Passwords and security

    Your password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider, which means we cannot read it. Information is sent between your browser and the portal over an encrypted connection. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

    5. Cookies and local storage

    The portal uses small pieces of data stored in your browser:

    • Sign-in session to keep you signed in while you use the portal.
    • Theme preference to remember light or dark mode.
    • Remembered email (only if you tick "Remember my email") so we can show "Continue as…" on the sign-in page. Choose "Not you?" on that screen to clear it.

    You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

    6. Sharing your information

    We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the portal (for example hosting and account authentication), and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety and rights of our users and the portal.

    7. How long we keep it

    We keep your account information while your account is open. Chat conversations are kept for as long as needed to provide support and improve our service, then deleted or anonymised. We may keep limited records for longer when the law requires it.

    8. Your choices and rights

    Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

    • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
    • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
    • ask us to delete your account and information;
    • object to or limit certain uses of your information.

    To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm it is really you first.

    9. Children

    The portal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

    10. International transfers

    Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. When we transfer information, we take reasonable steps to make sure it stays protected.

    11. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as the portal changes. When we make an important change, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the portal.
