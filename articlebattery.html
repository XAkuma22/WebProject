    If your FruitPhone battery is draining too fast

    Published: September 2026  |  Knowledge Base ID: KB-1042

    Battery drain can occur due to intensive background activity, out-of-date PassionOS software, or apps running continuously in the background. Follow the diagnostic steps below to resolve rapid power drops.

    The solution, step by step

    1. Inspect battery usage

      Go to Settings > Battery to view battery consumption by app over the last 24 hours.

    2. Enable Low Power Mode

      Low Power Mode reduces display brightness and minimizes background system activity until you can fully charge your device.

    3. Update PassionOS

      Make sure your device is running the latest PassionOS version via Settings > General > Software Update.

    If your FruitPhone won’t charge or turn on

    If your device is unresponsive when plugged into power, work through these checks.

    1. Check the cable and adapter

      Try a different cable and power adapter, and plug into a wall outlet rather than a computer or hub.

    2. Clean the charging port

      Gently remove lint or debris from the port with a soft, dry, non-metallic brush, then charge for at least 30 minutes.

    3. Force restart

      While connected to power, press and hold the Side button and Volume Down button together for about 10 seconds until the Passion Fruit logo appears.
